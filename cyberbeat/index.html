Rhythm runner

Cyberbeat

A cube rides a road that is built on the beat of the track.

Dash left and right, jump on the clap, and drive through a colour gate to repaint the cube — then sweep up every brick of your own colour. Nine original tracks, two modes, and a run that takes about a minute.

How a run works

One rule holds the whole game together: the bricks on the road have colours, and only the ones that match your cube belong to you.

◆

Colour gates

Drive through a gate and the cube takes its colour. You can catch a gate in any lane and at any height, mid-jump included.

▦

Your bricks, not theirs

Collecting a brick of your own colour scores a point. Hitting a brick of someone else's colour costs you one — or ends the run in Hard.

⇄

Dash and jump

Two inputs, both on the beat: a dash to the next lane and a jump on the clap. Every chart is written to the track, not generated at random.

★

Stars open tracks

A run is scored as a percentage: 50% earns one star, 75% two, 95% three. Twenty-seven stars are on the table, and tracks unlock as you collect them.

♫

Notes buy skins

Every run pays notes. Spend them on six cube skins and six road skins — nothing here is behind a paywall.

◉

Zen

Tap the eye on a track and it plays itself, hands off, with a ZEN badge in the corner. Good for learning a chart, or just watching.

Two ways to play

One switch above the track list, and the game remembers it. The charts are the same — what changes is the price of a mistake.

Normal

Ride the whole track, whatever happens.

  • A wrong brick costs one point and the run goes on
  • A blinking hand shows you the first jumps
  • Stars, records and notes for every finish

Hard

One wrong brick and the track starts over.

  • Attempts are counted, and the run restarts straight away
  • Only a finished run pays — and it pays double notes
  • A crown on the card for every track you clear

Nine original tracks

Every track is written for this game, and the road is cut to its beat. They are ordered by measured load, so the pressure climbs track by track.
