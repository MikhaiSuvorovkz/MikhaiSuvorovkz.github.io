# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **Deer Simulator: Animal Family** with package name `unimix.studio.deer.simulator.animal.sim.family`.

## Information We Collect

The game does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

To provide advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, and platform features, the game or its service providers may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app activity, diagnostics, and advertising interactions.

## Advertising and Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and Appodeal, for advertising and mediation. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Appodeal Privacy Policy](https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy)

## Children’s Privacy

The game is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services may process technical and advertising data under their own policies and applicable controls.

## Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by service providers to operate the game, show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and diagnose crashes. Retention periods are determined by the relevant service providers and the purpose for which the information is used.

## Your Choices

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to reset or limit the use of your advertising identifier, manage personalized advertising, or restrict permissions through your device settings. You can also stop data collection by uninstalling the game.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.
